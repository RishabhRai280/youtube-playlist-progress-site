Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work on private playlists? +
Yes! Since the extension runs locally on your browser, it can read any playlist you have access to, including private and unlisted ones.
Where is my data stored? +
100% on your device. We use Chrome's local storage sync. We do not have any database of your viewing history.
Is it free? +
Yes, the core features are completely free.
