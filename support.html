Quick Troubleshooting

Extension not appearing?

1. Click the puzzle icon (🧩) in Chrome's toolbar.
2. Find "YouTube Playlist Progress" and click the pin icon.
3. Refresh your YouTube page.

Progress not tracking?

Ensure you are on a valid YouTube Playlist page (URL contains `&list=...`). The extension works best on the full playlist view or within the video player with the playlist sidebar open.

Resetting Data

Open the extension popup > Click "Settings" (gear icon) > Scroll down to "Danger Zone" > Click "Clear All Data" to perform a hard reset.

Common Questions

Does it work on private playlists? +
Yes! Since the extension runs locally on your browser, it can read any playlist you have access to, including private and unlisted ones.
Where is my data stored? +
100% on your device. We use Chrome's local storage sync. We do not have any database of your viewing history.
